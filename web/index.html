UNDER THE HOOD

What it actually does.

The short version, in the order you’ll meet it.

01 /

Instant offline OCR

Shelf tag goes in front of the camera, price comes out. Runs on-device via ML Kit, so it still works inside a basement supermarket.

0.1s per read · no upload · works on printed and handwritten price signs
02 /

Department budgets

Six caps with a donut chart on the dashboard, so it’s obvious at a glance where the money went before you check out.

MEAT P680 · PRODUCE P140 · BEV P605 · 71% total
03 /

Per-kilo calculator

Produce, seafood, and meat sold by weight: price per kilo × weight, computed and added straight to the cart.

P240/kg × 1.25kg = P300.00 — no decimals lost, no rounding surprises
04 /

Pre-shopping list

Type your list before leaving the house. Every scan checks an item off by name match — including leftovers you forgot to grab.

AUTO-CHECK: scan matches list → item ticks itself → you keep walking
05 /

Store price trends

Save each trip with its store name — SM, Robinsons, Puregold, the wet market. Compare later which branch charges less for the same basket.

3 TRIPS SAVED · last trip: P1,425.50 · vs usual P1,510.00 — P84.50 cheaper
06 /

Local privacy, full stop

SQLite on your phone. No account, no registration, no uploads. If you delete the app, your data goes with it — and that’s the whole point.

DATA: stays on device · BACKUPS: your choice · ADS: 0
